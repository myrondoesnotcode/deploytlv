The short version

I started Deploy in May 2026 as a room for people building with AI in Tel Aviv. Five Deploy nights later, it's a community of more than 400 people, a weekly coworking, a startup event, and a show: founder stories on Instagram, long-form on YouTube, and the orange mic at the events worth being in.

Before founding Deploy, I produced media content for Aleph VC, including Invested with Michael Eisenberg, and worked on content campaigns for global brands including Disney, McDonald's and Duolingo.

I also teach Deploy Academy, practical AI workshops where people build something real in an evening. I've never worked as a developer. Everything I've shipped, I built with AI.

I'm from Tel Aviv and live by the beach. I speak English, Hebrew and Russian.

Bio for press and organisers

Myron Shneider is the founder and host of Deploy, an English-language tech media company based in Israel, covering the people, startups, and ideas shaping the future of technology. Deploy also runs Israel's fastest-growing English-speaking AI community, connecting founders, builders, and investors. Before founding Deploy, Myron produced media content for Aleph VC, including Invested with Michael Eisenberg, and worked on content campaigns for global brands including Disney, McDonald's, and Duolingo.

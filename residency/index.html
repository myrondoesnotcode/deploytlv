How it works

Three steps, from casting to your screen.

Cast the founders

We scout and cast 10 founders who clear the bar: $1M+ raised or $250K+ in revenue.* Then we film each one's interview before the house opens: who they are, what they're building, the goal they came for.

Those interviews become the first shorts, so people know the cast before day one.

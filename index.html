Deploy, in short

What is Deploy?

Deploy is an English-language media company telling the stories of Israeli tech and AI, based in Tel Aviv. We publish one-minute founder stories on Instagram, long-form episodes on YouTube, and cover the city’s tech events with the orange mic. We also run Israel’s fastest-growing English-speaking AI community: Deploy nights, weekly coworking and Deploy Academy.

Is everything in English?

Yes. Every story, episode, event and Academy class is in English, for locals, newcomers and international teams alike.

How do I come to a Deploy night?

Deploy nights are free and listed on Luma; the next one is in the guide above. Join the community to hear about each one first, and come to weekly coworking in Tel Aviv in the meantime.

Can Deploy cover my event or launch?

Yes. Event coverage starts from ₪3,000 in Tel Aviv: Myron on the orange mic, a finished reel and a collab post on @deploytlv. We also make founder films and long-form explainers, and sponsors can present our stories.

Who hosts Deploy?

Myron Shneider, founder and host of Deploy. Before founding Deploy, he produced media content for Aleph VC, including Invested with Michael Eisenberg.

Before you book

Questions

Do I need coding experience?+

No. That is who the class is for. The AI writes the code; you learn how to direct it and how to get it online.

Do I need an idea?+

No. Bring one if you have one. If not, you start from one of the starter projects and the first twenty minutes turn it into something buildable.

What do I leave with?+

A finished project on a live public URL under your own account, the prompting guide that got it there, and an Academy certificate. Nothing is locked to us.

What should I bring?+

A laptop and its charger. That is the whole list.

What software or accounts do I need?+

Nothing to buy in advance. We set up the accounts and the AI tooling with you at the start of class — that is the first block of the session.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?+

Full refund if you cancel more than 48 hours before the class starts. Inside 48 hours we will move you to the next cohort.

Is this available for companies?+

Yes. Academy for Teams runs the same workshop privately for a team, adapted around that team’s actual workflows.

Will there be more Academy courses?+

Yes — video editing, automation and building an AI tool are all in the catalog above. Join a waitlist and you will hear when a date lands.

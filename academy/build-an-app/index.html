01 / The four evenings

Something live every week

  1. Week 1 · Mon 16 Nov
    Idea to first version
    Cut your idea down to the one thing it has to do. Set up, build the first screen, and put it online the same night.
  2. Week 2 · Mon 23 Nov
    Make it remember
    Save data so the app keeps what people put into it, and add sign-in if it needs one. This is what turns a page into an app.
  3. Week 3 · Mon 30 Nov
    Make it feel like an app
    Phone layout, design, and installing it on your home screen. Then hand it to three people before next week.
  4. Week 4 · Mon 7 Dec
    Ship and demo
    Fix what your testers found, polish, and show the room a live app with real users.

05 / FAQ

Before you book

Q_01When does it start?

Monday 16 November, 19:00 – 21:00, at Pinsker St 11, Tel Aviv-Yafo. Ten seats.

Q_02Is the course taught in English?

Yes. Every Deploy Academy class is taught in English, in person in Tel Aviv. You don’t need any Hebrew.

Q_03Do I need coding experience?

No. The AI writes the code. Over four evenings you learn how to direct it, test what it made, and get it online.

Q_04What kind of app will I build?

A web app: it lives at its own URL, works on any phone or laptop, and installs on a phone’s home screen like any other app. It is not published to the App Store or Google Play in the course.

Q_05Should I take Academy 101 first?

It helps but it isn’t required. 101 is one evening and you leave with a website; 201 starts from the same setup and goes four weeks further.

Q_06What if I miss an evening?

Tell us beforehand and we help you catch up at the start of the next one. Your app stays online either way.

Q_07What software or accounts do I need?

Claude Code, which comes with a Claude Pro plan or above, installed before the first evening. Everything else, including where the app is hosted and stores its data, we set up with you in class.

Q_08What if not enough people sign up?

Each class needs a full enough room to run. If yours doesn’t fill, we tell you ahead of time and you choose: move to the next run, or a full refund.

Q_09What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Full refund if you cancel more than 48 hours before the class starts. Inside 48 hours we will move you to the next cohort.

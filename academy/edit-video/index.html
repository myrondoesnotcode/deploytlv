01 / The two hours

You edit during the class, not after it

  1. 0:00
    Film
    Shoot something short on your phone, or pick clips you brought. No footage? Use ours.
  2. 0:15
    Setup
    Get the footage onto your laptop. Claude Code installs ffmpeg and Whisper with you.
  3. 0:30
    Edit
    Claude transcribes your footage, you say what to keep, it cuts. Review, push back, recut. The longest block, on purpose.
  4. 1:10
    Captions
    Captions from the transcript, a vertical crop for phones, audio levelled.
  5. 1:30
    Export
    Export a finished file ready for Instagram, TikTok or LinkedIn, then make one more version.
  6. 1:50
    Screening
    Play it for the room.

05 / FAQ

Before you book

Q_01Do I need video editing experience?

No. You tell Claude what you want in plain English and it does the cutting. You learn how to direct it and how to judge the result.

Q_02Is this an AI video-generation class?

No. You edit real footage, filmed by you. AI is the editor, not the camera.

Q_03Where does the footage come from?

You film something on your phone in the first fifteen minutes, or bring a few minutes of your own clips. If you have neither, you use our sample footage.

Q_04What do I leave with?

A finished short video on your laptop, ready to post, and the prompts that made it so you can do the next one yourself.

Q_05What software or accounts do I need?

Claude Code, which comes with a Claude Pro plan or above, installed before the session. ffmpeg and Whisper are free and we install them with you in class.

Q_06What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Full refund if you cancel more than 48 hours before the class starts. Inside 48 hours we will move you to the next cohort.

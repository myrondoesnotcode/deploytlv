05 / FAQ

Before you book

Q_01Do I need video editing experience?

No. You tell Claude what you want in plain English and it does the cutting. You learn how to direct it and how to judge the result.

Q_02Is this an AI video-generation class?

No. You edit real footage, filmed by you. AI is the editor, not the camera.

Q_03Where does the footage come from?

You film something on your phone in the first fifteen minutes, or bring a few minutes of your own clips. If you have neither, you use our sample footage.

Q_04What do I leave with?

A finished short video, and more importantly the workflow: the tools installed on your laptop and the prompts to make your next video on your own.

Q_05What can I do with it after class?

Edit footage, transcribe and caption it, add titles and animated graphics, build stop-motion sequences, and cut new videos from clips you already have. The class covers the basics of each. After that it is practice, and every video gets faster.

Q_06What software or accounts do I need?

Claude Code, which comes with a Claude Pro plan or above, installed before the session. ffmpeg and Whisper are free and we install them with you in class.

Q_07Can you run this for my marketing team?

Yes. We run it privately for marketing teams at companies and startups, on your own brand, footage and formats. Team sessions are quoted per team, so message us or email hello@deploytlv.com and we will send a proposal.

Q_08What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Full refund if you cancel more than 48 hours before the class starts. Inside 48 hours we will move you to the next cohort.

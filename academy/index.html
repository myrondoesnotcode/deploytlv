Questions

Is it taught in English?

Yes. Every Deploy Academy class is taught in English, in person in Tel Aviv. You don’t need any Hebrew.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The AI writes the code. You tell it what you want.

Who is it for?

People who don’t code and want to build things with AI: founders, marketers, teachers, freelancers and people changing careers. Classes are in English, so they suit English speakers anywhere in Israel, including expats, new immigrants and international teams.

When do classes run? Is there a daytime class?

Academy 101 runs every Wednesday evening, 19:00 to 21:00, at Pinsker St 11, Tel Aviv. The next date is at the top of this page. Daytime classes are coming: choose “Daytime” in the form and we will tell you when the first one is set.

I live outside Tel Aviv. Can I still come?

Yes. Students have come in from other cities for it. The class is at Pinsker St 11 in central Tel Aviv. If an evening class is hard to get home from, choose “Daytime” in the form and we will tell you when a daytime class is set.

Do I need an idea?

No. Bring one if you have it, or start from one of ours.

What do I bring?

A laptop and charger, with Claude Code installed (it comes with a Claude Pro plan, paid to Anthropic). If it isn’t working when you arrive, we fix it together.

How is this different from a bootcamp or an online course?

It is two hours, in a room of ten, and you finish with something live. A bootcamp takes months and teaches you to write code; here the AI writes it and you learn to direct it. An online course leaves you alone when setup breaks; here someone fixes it next to you.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund up to 48 hours before class. After that, we move you to the next one.

Can I use Codex instead of Claude Code?

Yes. Academy 101 works the same way with OpenAI Codex as with Claude Code, so bring whichever you already pay for. Codex comes with a ChatGPT plan such as Plus; Claude Code comes with a Claude Pro plan or above.

Is there a longer course?

Yes. Academy 201, Build an app with AI, runs over four weekly evenings and you leave with a working app. The single-evening classes are the place to start if you have never built anything.

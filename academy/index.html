THE THINGS
PEOPLE ASK.

The questions that come up every time.

I’ve never written a line of code. Is this for me?+

That’s exactly who it’s for. The AI writes the code. What you learn is how to direct it and get the result online.

I don’t have an idea. Should I wait for the next one?+

No. Three starting points are ready on the day, and the first twenty minutes turn whichever you pick into something buildable.

I’ve barely used AI tools. Is that a problem?+

No. We start from the beginning, and ten people means someone can look at your screen the moment you’re stuck.

What do I need to bring?+

A laptop and its charger. Mac or Windows, as long as it runs a modern browser.

Do I need to set anything up in advance?+

Three free accounts, all of which take a minute: GitHub, which is what your project publishes from, plus Claude and ChatGPT. Making them beforehand saves you ten minutes of the two hours.

Do I need to pay for an AI account?+

No. The free tier of Claude or ChatGPT is enough to build and publish what we make. Free accounts do have usage limits, which is why you want both — if one slows down you move your file across and carry on. If you already pay for either, bring that one.

What language is it run in?+

English.

How does paying work?+

Nothing is charged when you apply. Once your spot is confirmed we’ll reach out with the venue and payment details. The seat is locked when payment lands.

What if I don’t finish in two hours?+

You publish at the ninety-minute mark, not at the end, so there’s room to fix things. Whatever state it’s in, it goes live.

What happens to my project afterwards?+

It lives on GitHub Pages under your own account, on a URL that’s yours. Nothing is locked to us.

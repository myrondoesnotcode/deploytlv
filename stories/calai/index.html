Cal AI15 colleges rejected him. Then he sold his app.

Founder story · posted 9 Oct 2026 · under a minute

On Instagram ↗

Who's whoZach Yadegari and Henry Langmack · Cal AI · bought by MyFitnessPal in December 2025

The story

What I say in the video, word for word.

A Jewish teenager got rejected by fifteen colleges. The same week, he told his camera: "We just did our biggest month: 2.8 million in revenue." Years earlier, he'd made a promise on YouTube: "I'm going to make one million dollars before graduating high school."

With a friend from coding camp, he built Cal AI. Snap your food, and it counts the calories. Senior year, the letters came.

"Harvard, rejected. Stanford, rejected. Yale, rejected. I was shocked."

He posted the list. Twenty-eight million views. Miami said yes.

By December, MyFitnessPal had bought his app. In April, on a Forbes stage, he had news for Miami: "And to you, Miami, I had a great time. But please take this as my resignation. I'm dropping out. Thank you, Forbes."

A note on this storyThe quotes are Zach's own words from his videos. Reports put his college-rejection post at 22 to 27 million views, and describe him as likely to leave the University of Miami.
